# Word-list provenance

The English vocabulary in `src/content.ts` was independently curated for
TypeChamp from ordinary English words. It was not copied from Monkeytype,
a dictionary database, or another application's word list. Individual common
words are factual vocabulary; this selection contains more than 200 entries.
